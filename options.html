Tabs & spaces

Tabs without an opener or an active space for their window start here.

Turn off to put new tabs at the bottom. Favorites keep the order you set in each Space.

Keep open bookmark tabs visible beneath collapsed folders.

Mirror space membership, names, colors, and tab order to Chrome groups. Turning this off stops future updates to those groups. Your spaces are saved separately.

Air Traffic

Route temporary tabs to a Space when you open a site or change pages. The first matching rule wins.

Use a hostname like example.com, an exact path, or a path prefix like example.com/projects/*.

    Recent Tabs & Quick Switcher

    Use your assigned shortcuts to jump between recent tabs and to search open tabs, bookmarks, and history.

    Chrome asks for access to all sites to show the switchers over supported pages. The switchers use separate windows when this is off or Chrome cannot place them over the page.

    Show page images when you use Recent Tabs. Shortcut previews stay on this device for up to ten minutes.

    Chrome asks for access to all sites to capture pages you view. The five latest previews stay encrypted on this device for the browser session.

    Separate domains with commas or spaces. Entries include their subdomains. Select Save excluded sites to apply your edits.
