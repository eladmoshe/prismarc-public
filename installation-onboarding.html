Set up your switching shortcuts

Use Quick Switcher to search tabs, bookmarks, and history. Use Recent Tabs to jump back to the tabs you were just using.

In Quick Switcher, type a page name, choose a result with the arrow keys, and press Enter.

  1. In keyboard shortcut settings, find PrismArc.
  2. Keep any shortcut that works for you. To set one, click the pencil next to the command and press the key combination.
  3. Return to this tab to see the saved shortcuts. With at least two regular tabs open, try each shortcut.

Keep your tabs in the sidebar

Click a tab to switch to it. Use Spaces along the bottom to keep different sets of tabs, like work and personal.

To keep PrismArc in Chrome’s toolbar, open the Extensions menu (puzzle piece) and click the pin next to PrismArc. The toolbar icon opens the sidebar.

Optional setup

Copy current tab URL

Currently set: Checking…

In Chrome's extension shortcuts, find PrismArc and edit Copy current tab URL.

Sidebar position

Under Side panel position in Chrome’s Appearance settings, choose which side to show the sidebar on.

Bring your bookmarks

Open the sidebar once to create the PrismArc bookmark folder. Then copy folders into it in Chrome's bookmark manager. Each folder becomes a Space and its bookmarks become pinned tabs.

Keep PrismArc on your toolbar

In Chrome's toolbar, open Extensions and click the pin beside PrismArc. Click the PrismArc icon to open the sidebar.
